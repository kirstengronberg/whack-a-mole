Request order for one round

  1. Player taps Start. The client calls POST /api/sessions and keeps the returned id.
  2. The 30-second round runs locally.
  3. Player taps Save score. The client calls POST /api/sessions/:id/finish with name, score, hits and misses. The reply includes the rank.
  4. The leaderboard loads with GET /api/scores?limit=10.
